ABOUT RLabs

Where Learning Meets Real‑World Innovation

RLabs is an innovation-driven initiative under the Rajagiri Centre for Incubation, established by the School of Computer Science. We bridge the gap between academic knowledge and industry expectations by providing structured, hands-on internship experiences.

Our mission is to transform students into confident professionals through mentorship, live projects, collaboration, and real-world problem solving.

“Relentlessly Towards Excellence.”
